# Subject: World History (Grade 6-8)

## Unit 1: Ancient River Valley Civilizations
Human civilization first developed around fertile river valleys because regular flooding provided rich agricultural soil, fresh water for irrigation, and natural transport routes.

Key Civilizations:
1. Mesopotamia (Tigris and Euphrates Rivers):
   - Located in the Fertile Crescent (modern-day Iraq).
   - Inventions: The wheel, cuneiform writing, Code of Hammurabi (one of the earliest written legal codes, based on retribution "an eye for an eye").
2. Ancient Egypt (Nile River):
   - Depended on the predictable annual inundation of the Nile.
   - Built monumental pyramids as tombs for Pharoahs (divine rulers).
   - Developed hieroglyphic script, papyrus paper, and sophisticated geometry.
3. Indus Valley Civilization (Indus River):
   - Cities: Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro (modern-day Pakistan and northwest India).
   - Renowned for urban planning: grid street layouts, advanced brick drainage systems, and public baths.
4. Ancient China (Yellow and Yangtze Rivers):
   - Known as the cradle of Chinese civilization.
   - Developed silk production, bronze casting, and early dynastic governance (Shang and Zhou dynasties).
